Support

Something broken, something confusing, or an idea for a line? Write to a real person:

confusedgamedevsupport@gmail.com

Tsugi is made by one person. Replies are not instant, but every message is read. If you are reporting a problem, the two things that help most are your device model and what you were doing when it happened — and, if a board looks wrong, the station name at the top of the screen.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and nowhere else. Tsugi has no account and no server — your times and your half-finished boards live in a single file inside the app. That means nothing to sign into, and it also means deleting the app deletes your progress. It is included in an encrypted iPhone or iTunes backup, so a restore brings it back.

I am stuck. Where is the hint button?

There isn't one, on purpose. Every board in Tsugi has been checked by solver to have exactly one solution, and every one of them can be reached by deduction alone — there is never a point where you have to guess and hope. If you are stuck, the piece you need is somewhere you have not looked yet. Try reading a row or column whose number is nearly satisfied: those constrain hardest.

Can I erase a piece?

Press and hold it. A ring fills around the piece, and when it completes the piece lifts. Track that came with the board is fixed and cannot be erased — that is what makes it a given.

What do the coloured squares mean when I tap a cell?

They tell you what that side is allowed to do. Green: you may connect this way. Red: you may not — there is a wall or a piece that does not point back at you. The line's own colour: you must connect this way, because a neighbour or a tunnel is already pointing at this cell.

How do I open the next line?

Earn at least one star at every station on the line you are on. The next line then joins the network map.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete and reinstall the app. There is deliberately no in-game "wipe everything" button, because an accidental tap on one would be unrecoverable.

Does Tsugi work offline?

Always. The app has no networking code in it whatsoever, so there is nothing to lose when the signal goes — which is convenient, given where most of it gets played.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. iPhone and iPad first; Android is planned.
