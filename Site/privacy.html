Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026 · Tsugi, by GorillaGonzalez

Tsugi collects no data about you. None. This is not a policy of restraint — the app has no networking code in it at all, so there is no mechanism by which it could send anything anywhere even if it wanted to.

What the app stores

Tsugi keeps one file on your device recording your best times per station and any board you have left half-finished. That file never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not readable by us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No cookies — the app is not a web page.

Sharing a result

When you share a finished station, Tsugi puts a short piece of plain text on your clipboard or into your device's share sheet. What happens to it next is entirely yours — the app does not know where it went and is not involved. The text describes the puzzle only; it never contains anything about you.

Your purchase

Tsugi is bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Those companies handle the payment and receive the transaction data; we receive only anonymous aggregate sales figures and never your name, address or payment details. Their handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Children

Tsugi is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children specifically. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data — under the GDPR, the CCPA or any similar law — apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request and nothing to delete. The only data Tsugi has ever touched is the save file on your own device, which is under your control and removed when you remove the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version gains an optional online feature — the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and any such feature will be opt-in rather than assumed.
