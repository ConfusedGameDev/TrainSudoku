A logic puzzle where you build a railway.

Each board is a grid with a number beside every row and column. The number tells you how many pieces of track go in that row or column. Lay one unbroken track from the entrance tunnel to the exit tunnel so that every number is right, and a train comes out and drives the route you built.

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

What it is

216 stations, 24 lines

Every board has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, so you never need to guess. Boards grow from 6x6 to 8x8, and later lines give you less track to start from.

Up to three stars per station

A fast time earns more stars. The clock can never fail you, so you can also ignore it completely.

Saves by itself

Progress is saved after every piece, even in the middle of a board. Close the game whenever you like and the track is where you left it.

No ads, no accounts, no data

Tsugi is a one-time purchase. It collects no data and never connects to the internet, so it works anywhere, including on a plane or underground.

The network

Ashenvale, Briarwharf, Calderwyke, Emberfell, Ironmere, Ravenscar, Saltmarch, Stonehythe, Thistlebourne and fifteen more. Finish the nine stations on a line and the next line opens on the map.

Languages

Tsugi plays in English, 日本語, Español and Français. Station and line names are proper nouns and stay as they are in every language, the way a real network map does.
